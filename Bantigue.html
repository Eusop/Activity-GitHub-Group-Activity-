About Notely

A small tool built around one idea: writing a note down should never get in the way of the thought itself.

Notely started as a simple question: why do most note apps feel more complicated than the notes we're trying to take? We wanted something quiet and minimal — a place to jot an idea, group related notes into notebooks, and turn any note into a checklist, without folders, tags, or clutter getting in the way.

This page is part of a group activity for Integrative Programming Technologies 2, built to practice collaborating on a shared project through GitHub.

✎ Simple

No learning curve. Open it and start writing.

🔒 Private

Your notes stay yours, organized the way you like.

⚡ Fast

Built to be light, quick to load, and quick to use.
